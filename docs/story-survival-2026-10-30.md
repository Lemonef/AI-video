## Provisional rail-story engine

One sentence: จ๊าบติดอยู่ในรถไฟที่จอดกลางเมือง ต้องพาคนในตู้ไปถึงจุดอพยพ โดยไม่เปิดทางให้ผู้ติดเชื้อเข้ามา และทุกทางออกมีสิ่งที่ต้องเสีย

- Goal: reach a staffed evacuation point, not merely stay scared.
- Geography: infected front carriage / initially safe middle / rear platform exit. Establish with a short overhead map; show distances and barriers, no impossible teleport.
- Resources: one working phone, a small bag, finite battery, physical doors. Only show a timer when a real event supplies the deadline; no arbitrary countdown because the format likes one.
- Human stake: an ordinary fellow passenger helps close the first barrier. Preserve that relationship for later choices rather than adding nameless victims each episode.
- No supernatural imitation or hidden ghost rules. Threat behavior is learned from visible cause/effect and stays consistent.

9–16s DECISION/ACTION: Jaab and another passenger pull the carriage divider shut before the rush reaches them. Reach/contact/load/door-stop must actually animate. They gain a temporary barrier; first concrete problem is paid off.

16–23s CLUE/LEARNING: a phone rings in the infected compartment; faces turn to it. Jaab silences his own phone. The rule is demonstrated once through sound/action rather than explained as a lecture. Do not imply actual first-aid claims about infection.

23–31s CHANGE OF PROBLEM: the front route is blocked, the rear opens onto a station. A clear map and POV show an apparently open platform, but an abandoned bag/drag trail disappears around its blind stairwell. Neither route is certified safe.

31–40s CLIMAX: barrier starts bowing under weight. The helper must hold it while Jaab decides: move the group out through the rear station now, or go forward to contact the controller and keep the group contained. Show what each option risks; no magical crack on an indestructible window solely to force a cliffhanger.

40–45s CHOICE: A ออกไปทางสถานี — leave the weakening barrier, enter an unverified platform. B ไปหาคนขับ — attempt communication/controlled evacuation, approach the infected end. End on a visible decision and a useful clue, not another unrelated jump scare.
