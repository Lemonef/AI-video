# Bangkok flood Part 3 (finale) — story + shot plan

Owner order 2026-11-01: finale, post 20:00 Bangkok. Audience voted **B — stall for time and secretly send the clip + location**. Finale rules from owner feedback: strong climax, **no A/B choice at the end** (open comment question only), Jaab off-centre with real angle changes (wide/medium/close, back-view depth walks, motivated inserts, modest pushes — the approach the owner praised on ghost Part 3), poses not stiff, no rain/background noise unless rain is on screen, drone only on tense beats, quiet gaps ≥16–18 dB under speech, menace line = quiet human threat (no monster voice). Callbacks that pay off: Part 1 stolen TV on the boat, Part 2 chair joke, Part 2 forgotten service door, 11% battery, the red-shirt / green-shirt thugs, the rescue-boat light.

## Narration direction (for the TTS)
Same warm Bangkok male narrator (Iapetus), brisk connected storytelling. Hook urgent and low. Rewind lighter. Count = held breath, slower. Ring = sudden. Chair = quick, a hint of triumph. Door callback = small satisfied beat. Signal/turn = rising hope. Caught = brisk. Sent = quiet. End = warm, gentle smile. Outro = direct question to the viewer.
