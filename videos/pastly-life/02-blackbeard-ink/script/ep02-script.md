# Ep02 script: How Long Would You Survive on Blackbeard's Ship?

Status: DRAFT v1 (2026-11-22). The voice-over is not recorded yet. Zen reads it, then it gets locked.
Facts: `D:/second-brain/research/channels/2026-11-22-ep02-blackbeard-facts.md`. F-numbers below point to its rows.
Length: 957 spoken words = about 7 min at Ink pace (~140 wpm). The outline aimed for 10 min; Zen decides whether to grow it. Voice: second person, present tense.

**Title promise:** the hook answers "how long". Almost nobody on deck lives to see summer. The ending shows the only two ways out.
**Loops:**
- L1: why is he so calm? Closed at beat 11 (the powder-room plan).
- L2: who out of about 20 men walks away? Closed at beat 13.

**Topic check:** pirate survival POV is a proven theme. Ink's survive-eras videos get 2.9x (v2 hunt). Our angle is one real day and one real ship, told minute by minute, with the real survivor count. It is not a general "pirate life" list.

**Disputed facts:** each one is said as "one account says" or "legend says". Never state them flat:
- the night-before quote
- the hat matches
- the shouted lines
- the blow-by-blow of the fight
- the hanged count
- the powder-room man's name, which is never said

## 1 · 0:00-0:20 · Hook (L1 + L2 open)
> You have less than a day to live.
> You're one of about twenty men on Blackbeard's ship. By next summer, almost every one of you will be dead or hanged. Only two kinds of men walk away. Stay to the end and you'll know if you're one of them.
> And the strangest part? Tonight, Blackbeard isn't worried at all.

Picture: deck at night, you (crew stickman) mid-shot, red X count row of 20 faces, 18 go grey.

## 2 · 0:20-0:50 · The night before
> It's the 21st of November, 1718. A small sloop is anchored in a quiet inlet off North Carolina.
> There's rum, there's laughing, there are guests from town on board. Someone asks the captain: does your wife know where you buried your money?
> One account says he laughed: "Nobody but me and the devil knows where it is."
> Spoiler: no treasure of his has ever been found. The ship's hold is full of cocoa and cotton.

Explain card: "sloop = small fast boat with one mast". Caption on the quote: "Johnson's 1724 book, the only source".
F10, F12, F11 (the guests set up the Odel payoff)

## 3 · 0:50-1:30 · Why he's so calm (turn 1)
> Here's why he's relaxed. Five months ago he took a royal pardon. That's official forgiveness: stop pirating, and the crime is wiped.
> He didn't stop. But the governor of North Carolina is his friendly neighbour. So who's going to come for him here?
> Yes, the One Piece villain is named after him. This is the real one.
> What he doesn't know: one state over, Virginia's governor is paying for two boats out of his own budget to come and kill him.

Map card: NC and Virginia, red arrow down the coast. Explain card: "pardon".
F5, F13, F14

## 4 · 1:30-2:30 · Count the faces (turn 2)
> Count the men around you. Nineteen. Maybe twenty-five.
> Where's everyone else? His sailing master, Israel Hands, and about two dozen men are in town, miles away. Nobody is on lookout.
> You don't get wages here. You get a share of the loot: one for you, two for the captain. Your quartermaster is the man the crew voted in to split it fairly.
> Lights out at eight. Keep your gun clean. And tonight, nobody keeps watch.

Count row: 19 stickmen with a "?" on 6 more. Split card: ship vs town, Hands on the town side. Explain card: "quartermaster = elected number two".
Note: the rules come from other crews' written rules. VO says "pirate crews like yours", never "Blackbeard's rules".
F8, F9, F31, F32, F33

## 5 · 2:30-3:20 · Dawn: two sails (turn 3)
> Dawn. Mist on the water. And there's a sail. Then a second one.
> Two small boats, about sixty navy sailors. They stopped last night outside the inlet because they didn't know the sandbanks. Now they're coming in.
> Their leader is Lieutenant Robert Maynard. And they have no legal right to be here. Virginia has no power in North Carolina. They came anyway.

Scene: you squint from the rail. Count card: 20 vs 60.
F13, F14, F15, F16

## 6 · 3:20-4:10 · The face is the weapon
> Blackbeard gets ready. And this is what made him famous.
> One account says he tucked slow-burning fuses under his hat, so smoke curls around his face. Three pairs of pistols across his chest.
> The plan is simple: look so terrifying that people give up without a fight. And it usually works. One historian says that until today, he may never have killed anyone.
> Today that changes.

Scene: Blackbeard lights the match. The smoke stays at the hat, not in the beard (F7). Explain card: "slow match = rope that burns for hours".
F6, F7, F35

## 7 · 4:10-4:50 · Stuck on the sand (turn 4)
> You cut the anchor rope and run for open water. Then a jolt. Your ship sits still.
> You're stuck on a sandbar, a ridge of sand under the water. But so are they. One navy boat throws its heavy ballast overboard just to float free.
> Everybody is stuck, and the gap is closing.

Explain cards: "sandbar" and "ballast = heavy weight in the bottom of the boat".
F17

## 8 · 4:50-5:20 · The shouting
> Across the water, Blackbeard shouts: who are you?
> Maynard points at his flag: we're no pirates.
> One account says Blackbeard yelled back that he'd show no mercy and expect none. No prisoners.

Two-shot across water. Caption: "the words are from one 1724 book".
F21

## 9 · 5:20-6:00 · Broadside (turn 5)
> Then your crew fires everything on one side of the ship at once. That's a broadside.
> It's a massacre. Around twenty navy men are hit on the closer boat, nine more on the other one, and its captain is dead.
> For a moment it looks like you win. You might live to see lunch.

Scene: flash and smoke. Count row of the navy: a third go grey. Explain card: "broadside".
F18

## 10 · 6:00-6:45 · An empty deck
> The smoke clears. On Maynard's boat, the deck looks almost empty. Bodies, smoke, nobody moving.
> You throw bottles stuffed with gunpowder, homemade grenades. Then you throw hooks across and pull the two boats together.
> Blackbeard laughs: they're finished, let's go.
> You jump across.

Scene: grappling hooks. Explain card: "grenade = bottle of powder + fuse".
F19, F20

## 11 · 6:45-7:30 · The trap (turn 6, L1 closes)
> The hatches burst open. Maynard hid most of his men below deck the whole time.
> Now it's even, and you're on their ship.
> And here's why Blackbeard was so calm. Back on your ship, one man sits in the gunpowder room with a lit fuse. His order: if we lose, blow the ship up. Nobody gets taken alive.

Scene: hatch pop-ins, sailors stream up. Insert: the powder room, a match by the barrels.
Do NOT name the powder-room man ("Black Caesar" is a later legend).
F19, F26

## 12 · 7:30-8:15 · Blackbeard vs Maynard
> Blackbeard and Maynard fire their pistols at each other. Then swords.
> Blackbeard's blade snaps Maynard's sword. He swings again, and a sailor behind him cuts his neck.
> He keeps fighting. When he finally drops, Maynard counts five bullets in him and about twenty cuts.

Scene: duel, sword snap, then a count card: "5 shots · 20 cuts". The fight stays readable, no gore.
F22, F23

## 13 · 8:15-8:50 · The bowsprit (turn 7)
> They cut off his head and hang it from the pole at the front of their boat. The body goes over the side.
> Legend says the headless body swam around the ship before it sank. No one who was there ever said that.
> And the powder room? The fuse never touches the powder. Men stop him in time.

Scene: silhouette of the head on the bowsprit, wide and far away. Red X on the legend card. Explain card: "bowsprit".
F24, F25, F26

## 14 · 8:50-9:30 · So how long did you survive? (L2 closes, title payoff)
> So, how long do you last?
> If you were on that deck, you're either dead by noon or in chains. Sixteen men go to Virginia for trial. No jury, just judges. Fourteen are sentenced to death in March. About a dozen hang.
> Two men walk away.
> Samuel Odel says he was only a guest at last night's party. He's freed.
> Israel Hands was in town. He wasn't even on the ship. He gets a pardon.
> So the only way to survive Blackbeard's ship? Don't be on it.

Count row: 20 men. Most go grey with a red X. Two stay coloured, labelled ODEL (a party glass) and HANDS (a town house).
F11, F28, F29, F9

## 15 · 9:30-9:45 · The reward
> And Maynard, the man who won? His crews split four hundred pounds, and they waited four years to get it. He never got a promotion.

Coin row. "4 years" on a calendar.
